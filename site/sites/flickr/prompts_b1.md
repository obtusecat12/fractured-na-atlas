Shared style (put at the start of every message):
"Please make these as separate images, one per prompt, each 4:3 landscape. They should look like real amateur snapshots that ordinary people uploaded to Flickr in 2009 — not professional, not cinematic. Think a cheap point-and-shoot or an iPhone 3G: slightly crooked framing, the subject a bit off-centre or awkwardly cropped, flat harsh on-camera flash indoors or muddy available light, blown-out highlights, visible JPEG noise and softness, a little motion blur, colours slightly off. Everything (cars, clothes, phones, TVs, shops) must be correct for 2009 in the United States. Do not include any readable text, logos, brand names, watermarks, date stamps or recognizable real people."

Overhead fluorescent light, greenish cast, shot quickly on a phone at hip height.
